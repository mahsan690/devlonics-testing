A careers page is a page on your website that sells your company to the technicians you want to hire. With labor shortages limiting growth for nearly a quarter of home service businesses, it's one of the cheapest recruiting tools you have. Good candidates check your website before they apply, just as customers do.

## How tight is the labor market?

Jobber's [2026 Home Service Trends Report](https://www.getjobber.com/home-service-trends-report/), a survey of 1,050 US owners, found:

- **23%** say labor shortages limit their growth.
- **30%** say specialized trade workers are the hardest role to fill, followed by crew leaders and supervisors (19%) and installers and field staff (13%).

The outlook isn't loosening. The Bureau of Labor Statistics projects [11% growth for HVAC mechanics and installers from 2025 to 2035, with about 40,600 openings a year](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/heating-air-conditioning-and-refrigeration-mechanics-and-installers.htm). The median wage was $61,010 in May 2025.

## Why your website matters to candidates

Job boards bring applicants in, but most will look you up before applying or accepting an interview. They want to know whether you're a real, stable company, what the work is like and whether they'd fit. A missing or outdated careers page sends good techs to the competitor who answers those questions.

## What to include on a careers page

### The basics, up front

- **Open roles,** each with its own section or page.
- **Pay ranges** and how pay works (hourly, commission, bonuses).
- **Schedules:** typical hours, on-call rotation, weekends.
- **Benefits:** health insurance, retirement, paid time off, vehicle, tools, phone.

### What makes you different

- **Training and growth:** ride-alongs, certifications you pay for, paths to lead tech or manager.
- **Equipment:** newer vans, good tools, tablets and software that make the job easier.
- **Culture:** how you treat people, in their words, not yours.

### Proof

- **Photos and short videos** of your actual team, vans and jobs.
- **Quotes from current technicians** about why they stay.
- **How long people stay,** if you're proud of your retention.

### An application that takes two minutes

Most candidates apply from a phone. Ask for name, phone, email, role, years of experience and licenses, with an optional resume upload. Send each application straight to the hiring manager's phone, and reply within a day.

## Make it findable

- **Link it from your main menu and footer** ("Careers" or "Join our team").
- **Use clear job titles** people search for, such as "HVAC service technician," with your city.
- **Add JobPosting structured data** so roles can appear in Google's job search experience.
- **Share openings on your Facebook page** and in local groups where allowed.

## Recruiting beyond the trades

The same principles apply to home care agencies, where caregiver turnover is a constant challenge, and to cleaning and landscaping crews. Show the pay, the schedule, the people and an easy way to apply.
