A good review request is short, personal, sent the same day the job is finished and includes a direct link to your review form. Below are copy-ready texts and emails for home service businesses, plus how to get your Google review link and the rules that keep you out of trouble.

## Get your Google review link

Google lets you [create a link or QR code](https://support.google.com/business/answer/16816815?hl=en) that opens your review form directly:

1. Open your Business Profile and go to **Read reviews**.
2. Select **Get more reviews**.
3. Copy the link, or download the QR code (QR codes can only be created on a computer browser).

Customers [must be signed into a Google account](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122) to leave a review, which is one more reason to send the link to the phone they already use.

## The rules, in one minute

- **Ask everyone.** Google's policies prohibit [selectively asking only happy customers](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114), the practice often called review gating.
- **No incentives.** Google says offering free or discounted goods or services in exchange for reviews is ["strictly prohibited"](https://support.google.com/business/answer/16816815?hl=en). The FTC's rule also bans [buying reviews](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials).
- **Real customers only.** No reviews from staff or family.
- **Texting needs consent and registration.** Only text customers who gave you their number for the job, include a way to opt out, and make sure your business number is registered for texting with your provider.
- **Skip Yelp requests.** Yelp tells businesses [not to ask for reviews](https://searchengineland.com/yelp-increasingly-cracking-review-solicitation-across-internet-286290) at all.

## Text templates

Keep texts under about 300 characters, use the customer's name and the technician's name, and put the link last.

**Same day, after a repair**

> Hi Maria, it's Jake from ABC Plumbing. Thanks for having us out today, glad the water heater's running again! If you have a minute, a quick Google review helps our small team a lot: [link]

**Same day, after an install or bigger job**

> Hi David, thank you for choosing ABC Roofing for your new roof. It was a pleasure working with you. Would you share a few words about your experience on Google? [link] Thanks, Lisa

**After a cleaning visit**

> Hi Emma, your home's all done! Thanks for trusting Sparkle Cleaning. If you were happy with today's clean, we'd love a quick Google review: [link]

**Maintenance or tune-up visit**

> Hi Robert, thanks for having Mike out for your AC tune-up today. If you have a moment, a Google review helps neighbors find us: [link]

**Friendly reminder (send once, 3 to 4 days later)**

> Hi Maria, Jake from ABC Plumbing again. No pressure at all, but if you get a chance to leave a quick review, here's the link: [link]. Thanks again!

## Email templates

Use email for commercial clients, property managers and long projects.

**Subject:** Thank you from ABC Builders

> Hi Karen,
>
> Thank you for choosing us for your kitchen remodel. It was a pleasure working with you and your family over the past few weeks.
>
> If you have a couple of minutes, we'd be grateful for an honest Google review. It helps other homeowners decide whether we're the right fit:
>
> [Leave a review]
>
> If anything isn't right, please reply to this email or call me directly on (555) 010-0000.
>
> Thanks again,
> Tom, ABC Builders

## Leave-behind cards and QR codes

A small card with your QR code, left on the counter or handed over at the end of the visit, works well for older customers who don't text much. Add one line: "Happy with today's work? Scan to leave a Google review."

## Timing by trade

| Trade | Best moment to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Plumbing, HVAC repair | Same day, once the problem is fixed |
| Roofing, remodeling | Final walkthrough or final payment |
| Cleaning | After the first clean, then occasionally for regular clients |
| Landscaping | After a visible result: install, spring cleanup |
| Home care, clinics | After a positive milestone, following your privacy rules |

## Make it automatic

The businesses with the most recent reviews don't remember to ask; their system does it. Connect the request to the moment a job is marked complete in your field service software or CRM, send the text within an hour and the one reminder three or four days later, and stop if they've already left a review.
