Facebook is the second-biggest source of leads for home service businesses, after referrals. It works because it's where neighbors recommend businesses to each other, where people see the work you've done, and where you can run tightly targeted local ads. Here's what works in 2026 and what to skip.

## Why Facebook still matters

- **Reach:** [Pew Research's 2025 survey](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/11/20/americans-social-media-use-2025/) found **71%** of US adults use Facebook, second only to YouTube (84%). Instagram is at 50% and TikTok at 37%.
- **Leads:** Jobber's [2026 Home Service Trends Report](https://www.getjobber.com/home-service-trends-report/) found **32%** of home service owners name Facebook as a lead source, behind only referrals and repeat customers (59%).

Facebook is essentially a referral engine: it's where people ask for recommendations and check whether a business is real.

## 1. Set up your Business Page properly

- Use the same business name, phone number, website and service area as your Google Business Profile and website.
- Add your hours, services and price ranges where possible.
- Turn on messaging and set up instant replies, but make sure a person follows up quickly.
- Pin a post with your best review and a clear way to book.

## 2. Post real work, not stock graphics

The content that performs best for local service businesses is simple:

- **Before and after photos** of jobs, with the town and a sentence about the problem.
- **Short phone videos:** a technician explaining a fix, a time-lapse of a clean, a walk-through of a finished deck.
- **Customer reviews,** shared with permission.
- **Your team:** new hires, anniversaries, training days.
- **Seasonal tips** that help homeowners, like freeze warnings for pipes or spring AC checks.

Two or three posts a week is plenty if they're genuine.

## 3. Be useful in local groups

Neighborhood and town groups are where people ask "Can anyone recommend a plumber?" Join as yourself, answer questions in your area of expertise and follow each group's promotion rules. Customers who are happy with your work will often tag you in recommendation threads, and that's worth more than an ad.

## 4. Run focused local ads

- **Target tightly:** your real service area, not a whole state.
- **Use one clear offer:** a seasonal tune-up, a free estimate, a limited-time discount.
- **Show real photos** of your team and work.
- **Send people to a page built for the offer,** with your number and a short form, not your homepage.
- **Track leads, not likes:** install the Meta pixel and conversion tracking so you can see cost per lead and per booked job.

## 5. Reply fast

Messages are leads. Jobber found more than 55% of customers expect a reply within the hour. Treat Facebook messages like phone calls; our guide to [speed to lead](/blog/speed-to-lead-home-service-businesses) covers how to make that manageable.
