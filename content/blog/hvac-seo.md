HVAC SEO is the work of making your heating and cooling company the one people find, and call, when they search "AC repair," "furnace not working" or "HVAC company near me." For most HVAC businesses that comes down to the Google map results and a website with a page for every service and main town.

Google says it ranks local results on [relevance, distance and prominence](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091). HVAC adds one twist: demand follows the weather. The company that prepares before the first heatwave gets the calls; the one that starts during it is too late.

## 1. Set up your Google Business Profile for HVAC

- **Primary category:** Usually "HVAC contractor." Google says to choose [the most specific category](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7249669), and that categories affect local ranking.
- **Additional categories:** A few that match work you genuinely do, such as air conditioning or heating. Don't add one for every service.
- **Services:** List each service separately with a short description: AC repair, AC installation, heat pump installation, furnace repair, duct cleaning, maintenance plans.
- **Service areas:** If customers don't visit you, [remove your address and add service areas](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481). Google allows up to 20, within roughly a 2-hour drive.
- **Hours:** List emergency hours only if someone really answers.
- **Photos:** Technicians, trucks, installs and finished systems, added regularly.

## 2. Build pages for how HVAC customers search

HVAC searches fall into three groups, and each needs its own page.

| Search type | Examples | Page to build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent repair | "AC not cooling," "furnace repair near me" | AC repair, furnace repair, emergency HVAC |
| Replacement | "new AC unit cost," "heat pump installation" | Replacement and installation pages, with price ranges and financing |
| Ongoing care | "AC tune up," "HVAC maintenance plan" | Maintenance plan page with what's included |

Add a page for each main town you serve, with real local content: neighborhoods, common system types in the area, reviews from local customers. Our guide to [service area pages](/blog/service-area-pages-seo) explains how to do this without thin, copy-pasted pages.

## 3. Get ahead of the seasons

Pages take weeks to be crawled and ranked. Plan your content calendar around the weather:

- **Late winter to early spring:** AC tune-ups, AC replacement, heat pumps
- **Late summer:** furnace tune-ups, heating repair, heat pump heating
- **Shoulder seasons:** maintenance plans, indoor air quality, repair-or-replace guides

Our guides to [HVAC marketing in 2026](/blog/hvac-marketing-2026) and [slow season marketing](/blog/slow-season-marketing-home-services) cover the rest of the seasonal plan.

## 4. Answer the questions homeowners actually have

The highest-value HVAC searches are questions: "should I repair or replace my AC," "how long does a furnace last," "is a heat pump worth it." Answer them honestly on your site, with price ranges where you can.

Refrigerants are a good example. Under the EPA's Technology Transitions program, systems using higher-GWP refrigerants and [manufactured before January 1, 2025 could be installed only until January 1, 2026](https://hardinet.org/posts/advocacy/epa-moves-install-date-for-residential-and-light-commercial). Existing systems can still be serviced. Plenty of homeowners don't know that, and a short, accurate page earns trust before they call.

Financing belongs on replacement pages too. Our guide to [offering financing on your website](/blog/offer-financing-on-your-website) covers how to show it.

## 5. Keep reviews coming all year

Reviews drive prominence and decide who gets the call. [BrightLocal's 2026 survey](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) found **74%** of consumers only care about reviews from the last three months, so a summer full of reviews followed by a quiet winter shows up in your profile.

Ask after every repair, install and tune-up, and reply to every review. Maintenance visits are an easy, steady source.

## 6. Earn local links and keep listings consistent

- Manufacturer dealer locators, if you're a certified dealer
- Local chambers, trade associations and utility rebate program directories
- Builders and property managers you work with
- The same name, address and phone number on Apple Maps, Bing Places, Yelp, the BBB and Nextdoor

## 7. Track it across a full year

Monthly numbers in HVAC swing with the weather. Compare each month with the same month last year, not the month before.

- Calls, direction requests and website clicks from your Google Business Profile
- Search Console impressions and clicks for your service pages
- Calls and forms on your website, by page
