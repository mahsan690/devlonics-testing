Local SEO for a multi-location home service business means making each branch rank in its own area. In practice, every real location gets its own verified Google Business Profile, its own page on your website, its own local phone number and its own steady flow of reviews, all under one consistent brand.

Here's how to set it up without the duplicate listings and suspensions that catch many growing companies.

## One location, one profile

Google ranks local results on [relevance, distance and prominence](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091). Distance is measured location by location, so a single profile for a company with five branches can only rank well around one of them.

The rule: each real location where your team works and serves customers gets its own profile. Towns you serve from an existing branch go in that branch's [service areas](/blog/service-area-business-google-business-profile), not in a new profile.

## Bulk verification for 10 or more locations

Google offers bulk verification through Business Profile Manager. From its [bulk verification guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3217747?hl=en):

- **Add at least 10 locations** to your account first. With fewer, verify each one individually.
- **Only businesses that make in-person contact with customers qualify.**
- **Service-area businesses** can bulk verify only if their locations also serve customers in person during regular hours; otherwise they verify individually.
- **Use only your real business name,** with correct capitalization and no store codes or extra words.
- **Phone numbers** must follow the required format, and Google recommends a **local number over a call center number** where possible.
- Account emails that don't use your business's own domain need extra verification and take longer.

## Keep every location consistent

| Element | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | Identical across locations, no city or keywords added |
| Primary category | The same where the service is the same |
| Phone | A local number for each location |
| Website link | The location's own page, not the homepage |
| Hours | Each location's real hours |
| Photos | Real photos from that location's team and jobs |

Our guide to [Google Business Profile categories](/blog/google-business-profile-categories) covers choosing categories for each profile.

## Build a page for every location

Link each Google profile to its own page on your website, such as /locations/plano. Each location page should have:

- The location's address (if customers visit) or service areas
- Its local phone number and hours
- The team at that branch, with real photos
- Services offered from that location
- Reviews from customers in that area
- Recent projects nearby
- A map and directions, for locations customers visit

Avoid copying one page and swapping the city name. Our guide to [service area pages](/blog/service-area-pages-seo) explains how to make local pages genuinely useful.

## Reviews per location

Reviews are attached to each profile, so each location builds its own reputation. Send review requests from the branch that did the job, with that branch's review link, and reply to every review from the location profile. Our [review request templates](/blog/review-request-templates) work for every branch.

## Reporting by location

Track each branch separately every month:

- Calls, direction requests and website clicks from its Google profile
- Visits and leads on its location page
- New reviews and average rating

Comparing branches shows which local teams need help with reviews, photos or answering calls.

## Franchises and partnerships

Before a new location opens, agree in writing:

- Who owns the Google profile (usually the brand, with local managers as managers)
- Who replies to reviews and in what tone
- Who keeps the location page up to date
- What happens to the profile and phone number if the location closes or changes hands

## Common mistakes

- Profiles for towns where you have no office or team
- Several profiles for the same location
- Different business names on different profiles
- One central phone number on every profile
- Every profile linking to the homepage
