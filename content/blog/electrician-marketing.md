Electrician marketing works best when it's built around how people actually find an electrician: a Google search for a specific job ("panel upgrade," "EV charger installation," "outlet not working"), a look at the map results and reviews, and a call to whoever looks trustworthy and answers. That makes your Google Business Profile, your service pages and your reviews the core of the plan, with local ads and referrals on top.

## 1. Set up your Google Business Profile

- **Primary category:** "Electrician." Google says to choose [the most specific category](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7249669) and that categories affect local ranking. Add a few more only for work you genuinely do.
- **Services:** List each one with a short description: electrical repair, panel upgrades, EV charger installation, lighting, ceiling fans, generators, rewiring, inspections, commercial work.
- **Service areas:** If customers don't visit you, [remove your address and add up to 20 service areas](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481) within about a 2-hour drive.
- **Photos:** Your licensed crew, branded vans and clean finished work: tidy panels, installed chargers, lighting projects.

## 2. Build pages for the jobs people search

Generic "electrical services" pages don't rank for specific searches. Give each main job its own page:

| Page | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Panel upgrades | Higher-value job, often linked to EV chargers, heat pumps or renovations |
| EV charger installation | Specific searches from new EV owners |
| Generators and backup power | Storm-driven demand in many regions |
| Electrical repair and troubleshooting | Frequent, urgent calls |
| Lighting and ceiling fans | Smaller jobs that build a customer list |
| Rewiring and older homes | Safety-driven, higher-ticket work |
| Commercial electrical | Steady repeat work, if you do it |
| Each main town | Local relevance and local proof |

Each page needs a clear heading with the search words ("EV charger installation in Austin"), what's included, price ranges if you're comfortable sharing them, reviews from that kind of job and a big call button.

## 3. Lead with trust

People let electricians into their homes for work that's dangerous if done badly. Show:

- Your license number and proof of insurance on every service page
- Real photos of your team, not stock photos
- Warranties on your work
- Permit and inspection handling, explained simply

## 4. Make reviews routine

[BrightLocal's 2026 survey](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) found **97%** of consumers read reviews for local businesses and **74%** only care about reviews from the last three months. Ask after every job, the same day, and reply to every review. Our [review request templates](/blog/review-request-templates) make it easy.

## 5. Add local ads for immediate calls

SEO takes months. [Local Services Ads](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/7195435?hl=en) charge per lead rather than per click and appear at the very top of Google. Note that missed calls during business hours can be charged when the caller waits more than 20 seconds, so answering matters. Our comparison of [Local Services Ads vs Google Ads](/blog/local-services-ads-vs-google-ads) explains when to add search campaigns.

## 6. Build referral partners

Some of the steadiest electrical work comes from other trades and businesses:

- General contractors and remodelers
- HVAC companies installing heat pumps that need panel work
- Solar installers
- Property managers and realtors
- Home inspectors

A simple, reliable process for these partners (fast quotes, clear scheduling, photos when the job is done) brings repeat work without ad spend.

## 7. Market for hires too

Finding licensed electricians is often harder than finding customers. The [US Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/electricians.htm) projects electrician employment to grow **9%** from 2025 to 2035, with about **72,700** openings a year, and a median wage of **$63,190** in May 2025. A real careers page with pay ranges, schedules and photos of your team helps. Our guide to [careers pages that recruit](/blog/careers-page-hiring-technicians) covers what to include.

## 8. Answer every call

The best marketing is wasted on calls that go to voicemail. Use call forwarding, an answering service or an AI receptionist for busy periods and after hours, plus an automatic [missed call text back](/blog/missed-call-text-back) for anything that slips through.
