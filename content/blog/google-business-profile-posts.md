Google Business Profile posts are short updates you publish to your listing on Google Search and Maps: a recent job, a seasonal tip, an offer or an event. For a local service business they're a free way to show people looking at your profile that you're active, local and doing real work right now.

Here's how posts work, what to post and a routine that takes 15 minutes a week.

## The three types of posts

Google offers [three post types](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7662907?hl=en):

| Type | What it includes | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Update | Description, photo or video, a button with a link | Recent jobs, tips, news |
| Offer | Title, dates and times, plus optional coupon code, link and terms | Seasonal discounts, tune-up specials |
| Event | Title, start and end dates and times, description, button | Open days, community events, workshops |

## Where posts show up

On mobile, posts appear in the **Updates** or **Overview** tabs of your profile on Search and Maps. On desktop they appear in the **From the owner** section. Featured posts can show in place of reviews on mobile.

Posts older than six months are [archived unless a date range is set](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7662907?hl=en), which is why a steady rhythm matters more than a big burst.

## The rules that get posts rejected

From Google's post guidelines:

- **Phone numbers** in posts may be rejected if Google can't verify they're connected to your business. Use the call button instead.
- **Links** to malware, phishing or other harmful content aren't allowed.
- **Offers** need clear dates and terms.

And the usual rule applies: no offers in exchange for reviews.

## 20 post ideas for service businesses

**Recent work**

1. Before-and-after photos of a job, with the town
2. A tricky job your team solved
3. A big install finished this week
4. A customer review turned into a post (with permission)

**Seasonal and helpful**

5. A pre-season checklist (AC tune-up, gutter cleaning, winterizing)
6. What to do in an emergency before help arrives
7. Signs it's time to repair or replace
8. How to choose between two options customers ask about
9. A myth customers believe, explained

**Offers**

10. A seasonal tune-up or inspection price
11. A first-visit offer for new customers
12. Financing for bigger jobs

**Team and trust**

13. Meet a technician or crew lead
14. A new truck, tool or certification
15. Years in business or a milestone
16. Your guarantee or warranty, explained simply

**Local**

17. A local event you're sponsoring
18. A community project you helped with
19. Storm or weather updates and how to book an inspection
20. New towns or neighborhoods you now serve

## A 15-minute weekly routine

1. **Monday:** pick one good photo from last week's jobs.
2. Write two or three sentences: what the job was, where and what it solved.
3. Add a button: "Call now" or "Book."
4. Publish, and add the photo to your profile's photo gallery too.

Once a month, swap one update for an offer or a helpful tip.
