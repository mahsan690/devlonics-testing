Missed call text back is an automation that sends a text message to anyone whose call you don't answer, within seconds: "Sorry we missed your call, this is ABC Plumbing. What can we help with?" Instead of calling the next company on the list, the customer replies, and your team picks up the conversation by text.

It's one of the cheapest fixes in home services, because almost every business misses more calls than it thinks.

## How many calls are you missing?

A [Service Direct study of 1,000 recorded home service calls](https://blog.servicedirect.com/home-service-call-performance-report) found in-house teams answered only **66%** of calls. The business owners in the study estimated they answered **97%**. The study is from 2019, but it's still the best illustration of the gap: the phone rings while you're on a roof, under a sink or driving between jobs.

And customers don't wait. [Jobber's 2026 Home Service Trends Report](https://www.getjobber.com/home-service-trends-report/) found more than **55%** of home service customers expect a reply within the hour, and **28%** expect an immediate one.

If you run Google Local Services Ads, missed calls also cost money. Google [charges for missed calls](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/7195435?hl=en) during business hours when the caller stays on the line for more than 20 seconds. You pay for the lead whether or not you ever speak to them.

## How missed call text back works

1. A customer calls your business number.
2. Nobody answers, or the call goes to voicemail.
3. Within seconds, they get a text from the same number.
4. They reply with what they need, a photo of the problem or a good time to call.
5. Your team gets an alert and answers by text or calls back.

The text comes from your business number, so the conversation stays in one place, and the customer doesn't have to remember who they called.

## What the text should say

Short, human, specific. Ask one question.

**Standard hours**

> Hi, this is Sarah at ABC Plumbing. Sorry we missed your call, we're with a customer. What can we help you with? Reply here and we'll get right back to you.

**After hours**

> Thanks for calling ABC Plumbing. Our office is closed, but we read every message first thing at 7 am. If it's an emergency, reply URGENT and our on-call tech will call you back.

**With a booking link**

> Sorry we missed you! Reply with what you need, or pick a time for a free estimate here: [link]. Sarah, ABC Roofing

Things to avoid: long messages, sales language, and anything that sounds like a robot ("Your call is important to us").

## The rules for business texting

Texting customers is regulated, and the rules are tightening.

- **Register your number.** US carriers require businesses that text from regular 10-digit numbers to register under a program called A2P 10DLC. Twilio, for example, [blocks unregistered US traffic](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/blog/a2p-10dlc-registration). Your phone or software provider handles the registration, but you need to complete it.
- **Honor opt-outs.** If someone replies STOP, stop.
- **Keep it to the conversation they started.** A reply to their call is fine; adding them to promotional texts without consent is not.
- **Have a human follow up fast.** The text buys you minutes, not days.

This isn't legal advice; if you text at scale, check the rules with your provider.

## How to set it up

Most businesses don't need anything custom:

- **Business phone systems and VoIP apps** often include missed call text back as a setting.
- **Field service and CRM software** for home services frequently offers it, with the conversation saved on the customer record.
- **A custom setup** with a texting provider and an automation tool works when you want the lead sent to your CRM, logged in a sheet and routed to the right person.

Whichever you use, test it from your own mobile before going live, and check that replies reach someone who will answer them.
