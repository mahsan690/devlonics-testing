If your Shopify store gets visitors but few sales, the problem is rarely the platform. It's usually something shoppers hit on the product page or at checkout: surprise shipping costs, unclear delivery times, thin product details or a checkout that asks too much. Fix those before you spend another dollar on ads, because ads only amplify what the store already does.

## How big is the abandonment problem?

[Baymard Institute's research](https://baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate), based on 50 studies, puts the average documented cart abandonment rate at **70.22%**. Excluding people who were just browsing, the top reasons shoppers give for abandoning checkout are:

| Reason | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Extra costs too high (shipping, tax, fees) | 40% |
| Delivery too slow | 20% |
| Didn't trust the site with card details | 19% |
| Site required creating an account | 18% |
| Checkout too long or complicated | 17% |
| Website errors or crashes | 17% |
| Unsatisfactory returns policy | 13% |
| Couldn't see total cost up front | 12% |

Almost every reason on that list is fixable.

## 1. Show shipping costs early

Surprise costs are the number one reason. Show shipping costs or a free-shipping threshold on product pages and in the cart, not just at the last step.

## 2. State delivery times clearly

"Ships in 1–2 business days, arrives in 3–5" beside the buy button. If you dropship, be honest about timelines; slow delivery is the second biggest reason for abandonment.

## 3. Make product pages do the selling

- Several high-quality photos and a short video.
- Clear details: sizes, materials, what's included, care.
- Answers to common questions right on the page.
- Reviews near the add-to-cart button.

## 4. Build trust signals

Contact details, a real returns policy, recognizable payment badges, secure checkout and an About page with real people. Baymard found 19% abandon because they don't trust the site with their card.

## 5. Allow guest checkout and express wallets

Don't force account creation. Turn on Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal so returning shoppers can pay in a tap.

## 6. Keep checkout short and working

Remove unnecessary fields, test the full checkout on mobile, and check that discount codes, shipping rules and taxes work for every region you sell to. Website errors cause 17% of abandonment.

## 7. Write a returns policy people can live with

13% abandon over returns. A clear, fair policy, linked from product pages, removes the doubt.

## 8. Send abandoned-cart emails

Shopify's abandoned checkout emails recover some lost sales automatically. Send the first within a few hours, with the product image and a direct link back to the cart.

## 9. Fix tracking before you scale ads

Make sure Google Analytics 4 and your ad pixels record add-to-carts, checkouts and purchases correctly. Without that, you can't see which products and campaigns make money, and every budget decision is a guess.
