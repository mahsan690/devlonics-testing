Roofing SEO is the work of making your company the roofer people find and trust when they search "roofer near me," "roof leak repair" or "hail damage roof inspection." In roofing, trust is the whole game: homeowners are about to spend thousands, often after a storm, and they've been warned about crews who knock on doors and disappear.

Google ranks local results on [relevance, distance and prominence](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091). Here's how roofers improve all three.

## Why trust matters more in roofing

Roof jobs are big and getting bigger. [Verisk's 2025 US Roof Report](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/property/roof-claims-severity-hits-record-highs-even-as-storm-activity-falls-verisk-data-shows-577150.aspx) put the average residential roof replacement at **$17,631**, up 33% on the prior four-year average. And after a storm, homeowners hear from many contractors at once. [State Farm](https://newsroom.statefarm.com/state-farm-paid-over-56-billion-in-hail-claims-in-2025/), which paid more than $5.6 billion in hail claims in 2025, tells homeowners to beware of scams, check licenses and reputation, and call their insurer before signing anything.

So every part of roofing SEO doubles as proof that you're the safe choice.

## 1. Set up your Google Business Profile as a local roofer

- **Primary category:** Usually "Roofing contractor." Google says to pick [the most specific category](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7249669) and that categories affect local ranking. Add a few additional categories only for work you really do.
- **Services:** List each one: roof replacement, roof repair, storm damage, insurance claims help, metal roofing, gutters, commercial roofing.
- **Service areas:** If customers don't visit your office, [set service areas](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481) instead of showing your address. Google allows up to 20, within roughly a 2-hour drive. Out-of-state storm crews can't honestly do this; you can.
- **Photos:** Real crews, trucks with your name, and before-and-after shots of finished roofs, added every month.

## 2. Build the pages roofing customers look for

| Page | What it should include |
| --- | --- |
| Roof replacement | Process, timeline, materials, price ranges, financing |
| Roof repair and leaks | What you fix, how fast you come out, warranty on repairs |
| Storm and hail damage | What to do after a storm, free inspection, how claims work |
| Insurance claims | How you work with adjusters, what homeowners should and shouldn't sign |
| Metal, tile or other materials | The materials you're certified to install |
| Each main town | Local projects, photos and reviews from that town |

Each page needs a clear heading with the words people search ("Roof replacement in Tulsa"), your license number, proof of insurance, manufacturer certifications and a big button to book an inspection. Our guide to [service area pages](/blog/service-area-pages-seo) shows how to make town pages useful.

## 3. Publish before storm season

Pages take weeks to be crawled and ranked. If you build the hail damage page the week after the hailstorm, out-of-town crews running ads will already have the calls. Get storm, insurance and town pages live before your area's storm season. Our guide to [roofing leads after storms](/blog/roofing-leads-after-storms) covers the storm-week playbook.

## 4. Make reviews prove you're local and real

Reviews drive prominence and decide who gets the inspection. [BrightLocal's 2026 survey](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) found **74%** of consumers only care about reviews from the last three months.

For roofers, the best reviews mention the town, the type of job and the crew, and include a photo. Ask at the final walkthrough, and reply to every review. Our [review request templates](/blog/review-request-templates) include one for roofing.

## 5. Show trust signals everywhere

- License number and proof of insurance on every service page
- Manufacturer certifications and warranty details
- Years in business and your local address or office
- Real photos of your team, not stock photos
- A clear written estimate process: no pressure, no signing on the doorstep

These help people choose you, and the reviews and local links they earn over time feed Google's prominence factor.

## 6. Earn local links

- Manufacturer contractor locators, if you're certified
- Local home builder and realtor partners
- Chambers of commerce and the BBB
- Local news coverage of storm recovery or community projects

## 7. Track it

Roofing leads spike after storms and settle in between, so track monthly and compare year on year:

- Calls, direction requests and website clicks from your Google Business Profile
- Inspection requests and calls by website page
- Close rate by lead source, so you know which pages bring real jobs
